Fairs, schools and community

RC digger hire for fairs, schools and community events

A crowd-pulling attraction for spring fairs, school fetes, festivals and charity family days. Children queue up to drive real remote-control diggers, and parents stay to watch.

Request a quote

Great for

  • Village and community fairs. A drop-in activity that families come back to.
  • School fetes and PTA events. A themed attraction for construction and engineering topics, and a good draw for the day.
  • Festivals and fun days. Fully portable, indoors under cover or outdoors on a flat patch of ground.
  • Charity and inclusive family days. Fun that every child can join in, whatever their ability.

Recently enjoyed at

We were proud to bring the arena to a family day for the LimbBo Foundation, where children and their families took turns at the controls. See photos in our gallery.

How we run it

  • A protected sand arena, edged with caution tape so spectators have a clear view.
  • Short turns so more children get a go, with a friendly operator briefing each driver on the controls.
  • We arrive about an hour before opening to set up.
  • Battery powered machines, with a nearby socket helpful for recharging.

Planning an event? Tell us your date, location, opening hours and expected numbers. We will recommend a format and send a clear quote.

Common questions

How do you keep the queue moving at a busy event?
We use short turns, a clear starting line behind the caution tape and a friendly operator who keeps rounds flowing. Tell us your opening hours and expected numbers and we plan the format.
Can you run a drop-in stand all day?
Yes. For fairs and festivals we can run the arena as a drop-in activity over several hours. The timings and format are agreed in advance.
Is it suitable for children with different abilities?
Yes. We have enjoyed events where every child could take part, including the LimbBo Foundation family day. Tell us about any needs in advance and our operator will adapt the machines and the games.
How is it priced?
Community events are priced according to opening hours, location and the set-up you need. Send us the details and we reply with a clear quote.
