Not on the list?

Ask anyway. If your venue is further away, we look at the date, the size of the event and the travel involved, and reply with a clear answer. We would rather tell you straight than leave you waiting.

What we need from your venue

  • Clear floor space of about 3 x 5 or 4 x 6 metres, plus room around it for guests
  • Access about an hour before the event starts
  • If possible, a standard socket nearby for recharging controllers

Common questions

Do you travel outside these areas?
Often, yes, for the right event. Send us your date and location and we will tell you if it is possible and what the travel adds to the price.
Is there a travel charge?
The price depends on your location and timings, so travel is included in the quote we send. Nearby bookings are the most cost-effective.
Can you set up at hotels and venues I have never used before?
Yes. We need the venue contact, access time, the floor space available and a nearby socket if possible. We work in halls, hotels, offices, marquees, gardens and fields.

Is your venue in range?

Tell us the date and town or postcode. We reply with availability and a clear price.
