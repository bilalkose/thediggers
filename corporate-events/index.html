Where it works well

  • Christmas parties and dinners. Something more memorable than another quiz, and guests who would never normally try it end up queuing for a go.
  • Team building days. Communication, planning and a bit of pressure, without a trust fall in sight.
  • Family fun days. Children and parents play side by side. Our kids parties use the same machines.
  • Hotel and venue events. The floor is fully protected and we leave nothing behind.

What event planners need to know

  • Space: about 3 x 5 or 4 x 6 metres for the arena, plus spectator room.
  • Timings: we arrive about an hour before to set up and pack down afterwards. Play time is flexible and agreed with you.
  • Power: the machines are battery powered. A standard socket nearby for recharging controllers is helpful.
  • Operator: one friendly operator runs the whole activity and explains the controls.
  • Where: we are based in Worksop and travel across the Midlands and the North. See areas we cover.

Common questions

How many guests can take part?
Teams take turns in the arena, so it works for small teams and for large dinners. We plan the rounds around your timings and numbers, and guests who are not driving watch and cheer from around the caution tape.
Can you run it between courses at a dinner?
Yes. Short rounds fit neatly between courses or after the meal, and the arena can also be the main event of a team day. We agree the running order with you beforehand.
Do you need a lot of space?
We need about 3 x 5 or 4 x 6 metres for the arena, plus room around it for spectators. We protect the floor, so hotel function rooms, offices and marquees are all fine.
How are corporate events priced?
Corporate events are quoted individually because venues, numbers, timings and travel all differ. Send us the details and we reply with a clear price.
