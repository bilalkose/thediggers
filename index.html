How It Works

Simple for Organisers. Unforgettable for Guests.

Whether you're booking us for your event or we're running our own pop-up, the process is the same.

01

Enquire

Send us your event details through the form or call us. We confirm availability and the space we'll need.

02

We Set Up

We arrive and build the arena — sand, diggers, hazard tape and signage — typically within 90 minutes.

03

Guests Play

Participants choose their tier and operate the machines. We supervise, reset and manage the queue throughout.

04

Pack Down

At the end of the event we clear everything away and leave the area clean. No mess left behind.

How To Book Us

Two Ways to Work Together

Choose whether you want us to run the experience independently at your public event, or as an exclusive attraction at your private event.

Pop-Up at Your Event

We set up our arena at your festival, fair or market and operate independently, charging guests directly per session. You get a high-quality attraction that fills dwell time — at no cost to you.

  • We handle all payments and supervision
  • No upfront cost to the organiser
  • Revenue-share arrangements available for larger events
  • Branded signage and construction-zone theming included
  • Suitable for minimum 3-hour event slots

Exclusive Private Hire

Book the full experience for your private event — birthday party, wedding, corporate day or school fair. Your guests play for free; you pay a single hire fee that covers setup, operation and breakdown.

  • Full arena setup and branded theming
  • Dedicated operator supervision throughout
  • Sessions for your guests during the hire period
  • Seasonal challenge add-ons (Egg Hunt, Treasure Dig)
  • Half-day and full-day options available
  • Tailored to your event theme on request
