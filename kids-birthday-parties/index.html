How a digger party runs

Most parties run for around 2 hours of play, and we are happy to flex either way. Here is a typical flow:

  1. Set-up (about an hour before guests arrive). We build the arena, protect the floor and lay out the machines and the birthday banner.
  2. Welcome and safety briefing. Our operator shows the children how the controllers work and explains the rules of the arena.
  3. Games and free play. Between 3 and 6 children drive at a time. Group games might be a sand-moving race, loading the trucks or a team challenge. In free play the children dig and drive their own way while the operator helps.
  4. Treasure hunt. A favourite finale. Children use the machines to uncover hidden treasure in the sand.
  5. Pack-down. We clear the arena and leave the floor as we found it.

What is included

  • A friendly operator for the whole session
  • A protected sand arena (3 x 5 or 4 x 6 metres)
  • Real RC machines, with a bigger Pro excavator to try
  • Birthday banner, treasure hunt and games
  • Set-up and pack-down

What we need from you

  • Clear floor space of about 3 x 5 or 4 x 6 metres, plus room around it for guests
  • Access to the venue about an hour before the party starts
  • If possible, a standard socket nearby for recharging controllers

Common questions

How many children can play at once?
Between 3 and 6 children can drive in the arena at the same time. They can play group games and races, or free play with our operator helping. At bigger parties we rotate small groups so every child gets a proper turn.
Is it suitable for a party of 30 children?
Yes. Around 30 children works well when they are split into small groups. Parents and the children waiting stand around the caution tape, and the session usually runs for about 2 hours.
Can you run it indoors in a hall?
Yes. We protect the floor with heavy-duty sheeting and leave no sand behind. We need about 3 x 5 or 4 x 6 metres of clear space. Read how indoor set-ups work.
What is the difference between Little Diggers Farm and Pro Operators?
Little Diggers Farm is a farm-themed arena with small, gentle machines for ages 3 to 6. Pro Operators is a construction site with a crane, excavators, loaders and dump trucks, with bigger machines and team challenges for ages 6 and up. A bigger Pro excavator to try is included in both.
How much does it cost?
Little Diggers Farm starts from £360 and Pro Operators from £480, depending on your location and timings. Read what affects the price or send an enquiry for an exact quote.
