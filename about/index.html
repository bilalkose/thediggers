About us

About The Diggers Play

A portable RC construction play business from Worksop, Nottinghamshire, bringing real remote-control diggers to parties, fairs and corporate events.

Who runs it

The Diggers Play is run by Bill (Bilal Köse). Bill holds a degree in civil engineering and works as a professional artist, performing live sand animation across the UK as Sand Art UK. The Diggers Play is his second business: a digger arena that children and adults can drive for themselves.

What we do

We set up a protected sand arena at your venue and bring real remote-control excavators, loaders, cranes and trucks. Children aged 3 and up drive, dig and build with a friendly operator on hand, and the grown-ups usually can't resist a go too. Everything is portable, so we work in halls, hotels, gardens, fields and marquees.

  • Kids birthday parties with Little Diggers Farm (ages 3 to 6) and Pro Operators Site (ages 6 and up)
  • Corporate events such as Christmas parties and team days
  • Fairs, schools and community events

Where we work

We are based in Worksop and regularly cover Nottinghamshire, South Yorkshire, Derbyshire and Lincolnshire, and travel further by arrangement. See our areas we cover.
